Source
Scope. This library formalizes selected results of causal inference with explicit assumptions. It is not a complete formalization of the field; see Limits.

Milestones

Each entry is a commit on the main branch, oldest first, generated from the git history: the subject and the message of the commit, and the modules it added. Times are as recorded in each commit; the date is shown when it changes.

Every commit on the main branch passed the gate. The procedure page lists its steps: a source scan for placeholders and trust escapes, a build with no warning, and an axiom check that allows only the three standard axioms.

    Prove the global Markov property for finite acyclic graphs. 94ae45e

    A distribution that factorizes over a finite acyclic graph satisfies every conditional independence given by d-separation, for arbitrary finite sets and with no positivity assumption. Factorization, the local Markov property, and the global Markov property are equivalent. Includes the chain, fork, and collider patterns, an unfaithful distribution where an independence holds without d-separation, and a distribution that factorizes and makes two adjacent vertices dependent.

    Modules added: Graphical.GlobalMarkov, Graphical.GlobalMarkovCompleteness.

    Identification theorems require finite measures, non-null groups, and first moments only for the outcomes and dates they use. No anticipation is available at the unit level and in means. New results: conditional parallel trends with a general covariate, vanishing pre-period effects, the event-time aggregate as a weighted sum of observed contrasts, the two-period theorem derived from the staggered one, the two-way fixed effects coefficient on balanced panels by double demeaning, and an example where a lead coefficient is nonzero with no pre-treatment effect.   20. 20:24

    Add discrete-time survival analysis. 8ecddfa

    Survival, risk, and hazard for a discrete failure time, with the product formula and the result that hazards determine the law. Under independent censoring the product-limit functional of the observed law equals the survival function; a pair of models with the same observed law shows that survival is not identified without independence. Hazard ratios compared with survival and risk, including an example where period hazard ratios differ from one while survival is equal. The Kaplan-Meier estimator equals the survivor fraction when no observation is censored.

    Modules added: Survival.Basic, Survival.Censoring, Survival.Comparison, Survival.KaplanMeier.

  21. 20:25

    Add finite structural causal models. f8f9df7

    A structural causal model over a finite acyclic graph has a unique solution at each noise value and induces a distribution that factorizes over the graph; conversely every such distribution arises from a model. Interventions give the truncated factorization, compose, and change only descendants. Includes adjustment for direct causes, identifiability of effects with a latent confounder counterexample, and counterfactuals: consistency, composition, effectiveness, reversibility, the three-step evaluation, and two models with equal interventional distributions and different counterfactuals.

    Modules added: SCM.Basic, SCM.Counterfactuals, SCM.Effects, SCM.Intervention, SCM.Markov.

  22. 20:32

    Strengthen missing data and identify the mean from the observed law. 7f2b9d0

    Observed laws are interpreted only for admissible models, and the complete-case mean requires a response event of positive probability with an integrable outcome. Missingness at random in mean form is equivalent to conditional independence for a binary response and is stable under transformations of the outcome. Under missingness at random with a positive response propensity the full-data mean is a functional of the observed law. New: the weighting identity from integrability of the outcome alone, the normalization of the weights, the augmented identity in both robustness cases, and a counterexample showing that positivity is needed.

    Modules added: MissingData.Identification.
