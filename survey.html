Source
Scope. This library formalizes selected results of causal inference with explicit assumptions. It is not a complete formalization of the field; see Limits.

Literature survey

Every module states in its docstring which published sources it formalizes. This page summarizes those citations; the full list, with the mapping from each source to its Lean files, is the source register.

Sources

111 published sources are cited on the main branch: 18 books and 93 articles or essays.

Modules with a source

194 of the 245 modules cite at least one published source.

How the list is kept

The site generator reads every module docstring, checks each register entry against it, and reports any year in a docstring that no entry covers, so a new citation cannot be missed silently.
