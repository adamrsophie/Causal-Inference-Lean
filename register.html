Source
Scope. This library formalizes selected results of causal inference with explicit assumptions. It is not a complete formalization of the field; see Limits.

Source register

The 60 published sources cited in the module docstrings on the main branch, each with the Lean files that cite it. The register is part of the generator's notes, and every entry is checked against the source at each build: the first author and the year must appear together in the docstring of every module listed.

A citation means that the module formalizes a result, a definition or a setting from that source, as its docstring says. It does not mean that every result of the source is formalized. Titles and venues are given as in the docstrings where the docstrings give them. The survey page summarizes the register by area.
